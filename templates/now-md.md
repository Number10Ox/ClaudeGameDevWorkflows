# Now.md Template

> Copy this to your project as `Docs/Now.md` and adapt.
> Keep this under 30 lines for small projects, 60 for complex ones.
> Update after every session or design decision.

---

```markdown
# Now

> What Claude Code reads at session start. Keep this under 30 lines.
> Update after every session or design decision.

## Active Question

[What are we working on right now? One question or task. If nothing active, say so.]

## Mode

[DESIGN or EXECUTION]

## Layer

[Which layer? System / Mechanics, UI / UX, Representation / Narrative, Content, etc.]

## Maturity

[Project phase — e.g. Concept, Prototype, Alpha, Beta, Production]

## Current State

- [Bullet list of what exists and works right now]
- [Keep it factual — what's built, not what's planned]
- [3-8 bullets is typical]

## Non-goals Right Now

- [Explicit deferrals — things that are NOT being worked on]
- [Prevents scope creep and sets expectations]

## Open Questions (backlog)

- [Question 1 — captured but not active]
- [Question 2 — captured but not active]

### [Category Name] ([Layer])

- **[Question title]** — [Description of the design question, what needs to be decided, and what it depends on]
```

---

> **Adapt this:** The sections are the pattern. The content is entirely your project. Categorized open question subsections are optional — use them when you have 5+ questions that cluster by topic.
