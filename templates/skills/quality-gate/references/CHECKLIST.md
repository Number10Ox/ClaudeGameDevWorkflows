## Tips for Writing Checks

- Each check should be **pass/fail** — not "looks good" or "seems right"
- Reference specific terms, thresholds, or patterns from your source docs
- Group by concern (structural, semantic, consistency) not by source doc
- Keep under ~30 checks total — see constraint-audit.md if you exceed 15 per category
